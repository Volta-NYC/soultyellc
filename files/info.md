## VISUAL CONTENT & BRANDING

### Logo
The Instagram profile photo (and the logo printed on the round packaging stickers Tye puts on her takeout containers) shows a **stylized speech-bubble / talk-bubble shape** with the word **"SOUL"** drawn in hand-lettered, multicolor type — alternating red, orange, and yellow letters on a peach/light-orange background. There's also a secondary mark used on menu graphics: a simple **circle containing a crossed spoon-and-fork icon** above the "SOUL TYE LLC" wordmark. Some menu graphics swap the crossed-utensil circle for a **stylized chef-hat-with-utensils** mark.

### Color palette (observed across menu graphics, reels, and the logo)
- Warm orange / peach (logo background)
- Mustard yellow (recurring accent — Tye's signature yellow Soul Tye t-shirt in the AmNews photo, hat she wears)
- Bright red / tomato (menu headers)
- Dark forest green or chartreuse green (menu item type, accent)
- Black + white for the chef-hat icon
- Royal blue (used as accent in some menu graphics)

OBSERVATION: brand palette skews warm-soul-food (red, orange, yellow, green) — would look at home on a website that leans into a soulful, hand-lettered, food-stand aesthetic rather than a sterile clean-tech look.

### Recurring visual motifs
- **Round branded takeout stickers** sealing the foil/plastic catering containers. The stickers feature small thumbnails of Tye and her food.
- **Foil aluminum half-pans** and clear plastic catering trays (their signature packaging in photos).
- **Hand-lettered #SOULTYELLC text overlays** in IG reels — bold rounded white sans, often with a small black drop shadow.
- **Food close-ups** with shallow depth of field — saucy wings, oozy mac, bubbling braises, butter-topped cornbread.
- **Customer text-message screenshots** as testimonials (multiple posts).

## OBSERVATIONS / RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THE SITE (clearly labeled as my read, not their words)

OBSERVATION 1 — **Lead with the founder story.** The NY AmNews piece is the single best asset they have. A "Meet Tye" section that opens on the AmNews narrative ("After 13 years in retail…") will out-perform a generic "About us."

OBSERVATION 2 — **Treat the weekly menu as a featured graphic, not a static list.** Tye rotates the menu by hand on Instagram every week. The website should embed or mirror the current menu graphic, with a fallback "representative menu" list. Don't lock in items that disappear next week.

OBSERVATION 3 — **Build a distinct "Catering" landing page** — the bio already advertises catering and there is press-quality social proof (the "Everyone absolutely loved it" customer). The user just needs to provide actual catering details (minimums, pricing, lead time).

OBSERVATION 4 — **Use the customer-text-screenshot testimonials.** They feel authentic and are already on-brand. Either re-typeset them or display them as the actual screenshots she posted (with her permission).

OBSERVATION 5 — **Don't oversell hours.** The hours conflict (press said daily, menus say Tuesday–Friday) means you should confirm with Tye before publishing.

OBSERVATION 6 — **Brand palette and tone:** lean into warm hand-lettered + soul-food signaling — red, mustard yellow, peach orange, forest green; serif or hand-letter display type. Not a clean corporate look.

OBSERVATION 7 — **Reels are her main content asset.** An "@soultyellc on Instagram" embed module would keep the site feeling alive between client updates.
